# Abracadabrax Song Maker

Turn an idea into a finished song: write the lyrics with Claude, set style, vocals and length in a panel, then render the track in your Abracadabrax account.

## What it does

Describe the song you want. Claude writes the lyrics in the conversation, then prepares a song in an interactive panel where you review the title, the style brief (genre, mood, tempo, vocals), the lyrics, the model and the length, and see the credits required. When you ask for it, the song is rendered with vocals, or as an instrumental, in your Abracadabrax account and Claude returns the audio link.

**AI generation:** the songs are generated by AI music models run by Abracadabrax and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

New songs from a style brief and lyrics only. Covers, extensions and stem separation of an uploaded track are not supported: use the music studio on abracadabrax.app for those.

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the Abracadabrax MCP server at `https://abracadabrax.app/api/claude/song/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a song is free and works without an account.
- The first render asks you to sign in to Abracadabrax (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Rendering uses the credits of your Abracadabrax account.
- Claude shows an interactive panel where the client supports MCP Apps; Claude Code works with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_song_project` | Save the title, style, lyrics, model and length and show the credits required. Free, no sign-in. |
| `render_song_widget` | Reopen a prepared song in the panel. |
| `update_song_project` | Change the settings of a prepared song from the panel. |
| `generate_song` | Render the song. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_song_job` | Check a render and return the audio link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "Write and produce an upbeat indie pop song about a road trip with friends"
- "Make a warm acoustic ballad for my mom's birthday, male vocals"
- "Create a 2-minute lo-fi instrumental for studying"

## Data and privacy

Your lyrics, style brief and settings are sent to Abracadabrax and stored in your Abracadabrax account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the audio. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
