# Abracadabrax Image & Video

Create AI images and videos from a text prompt: review model, aspect ratio and duration in a panel, then generate them in your Abracadabrax account.

## What it does

Describe an image or a video. Claude prepares a project in an interactive panel where you choose the model (for example Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2 or Kling 3.0 Pro), the aspect ratio and the duration, and see the credits required. When you ask for it, the image or video is generated with an AI model in your Abracadabrax account and Claude returns the media link.

**AI generation:** the images and videos are generated by AI models run by Abracadabrax and its model providers. Nothing is generated until you ask for it, and the credits required are shown first.

Text-to-image and text-to-video only. Uploaded or reference media, image editing, background removal and image-to-video are not supported: use the workspace on abracadabrax.app for those.

## Setup

The plugin connects Claude to the Abracadabrax MCP server at `https://abracadabrax.app/api/claude/media/mcp` (Streamable HTTP).

- Preparing a request is free and works without an account.
- The first generation asks you to sign in to Abracadabrax (OAuth with PKCE; you can create an account on the same page). Generating uses the credits of your Abracadabrax account.
- Claude shows an interactive panel where the client supports MCP Apps; Claude Code works with text and links.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `prepare_media_project` | Save the prompt, model, aspect ratio and duration and show the credits required. Free, no sign-in. |
| `render_media_widget` | Reopen a prepared project in the panel. |
| `update_media_project` | Change the settings of a prepared project from the panel. |
| `generate_media` | Generate the image or video. Uses credits; needs sign-in. |
| `get_media_job` | Check a generation and return the image or video link. Read-only. |

## Example prompts

- "Create a product image of a ceramic mug in morning light"
- "Generate a short vertical 9:16 video of ocean waves at sunset"
- "Make a 16:9 cinematic shot of a red bicycle leaning on a white wall"

## Data and privacy

Your prompts and settings are sent to Abracadabrax and stored in your Abracadabrax account, and they are sent to the model providers that render the media. The plugin has no hooks or local scripts and sends nothing else.
